# Phase 2: Mapping Review

**Decision: Changes required. Mapping is not yet approved.**

## 1. Review Findings

- Reviewed 10 workbook sheets with 100 rows each.
- POC scope covers patient, visit, and diagnosis tables.
- 74 columns inventoried; 8 empty columns excluded and 66 retained.
- Candidate keys are complete and unique in the sample.
- Selected-table references are consistent.
- No full duplicate rows detected.
- Whitespace exists in text values.
- Available visit-date chronology checks passed.

## 2. Required Corrections

1. **Column names:** Match the loader's actual normalisation rules. Verify before creating destination tables.
2. **Waiting_time:** Keep as `VARCHAR(64)` until its meaning is confirmed. Do not assume it is a datetime.
3. **Numeric fields:** Confirm meaning and value ranges before assigning numeric types. Preserve codes as text where needed.
4. **Missing values:** Store as SQL `NULL`, not zero or `"nan"`.
5. **Whitespace:** Agree on trimming rules. Do not alter source values silently.
6. **QC fields:** Retain unless confirmed out of scope.
7. **Foreign keys:** Define only after destination names and DDL are confirmed.

## 3. Out-of-Scope Findings

Medication, operation-procedure, and laboratory sheets require separate chronology reviews. Unmatched references in other sheets may be due to the partial sample and do not prove broken relationships.

## 4. Next Steps

- Verify column normalisation against the loader.
- Confirm `Waiting_time` semantics.
- Review numeric types and trimming rules.
- Freeze the mapping.
- Proceed with database integration tests.
